How It Works

Three Steps. One Powerful Output.

No accounts, no installs, no complicated setup. Just open the app and go.

1

2

Score

What You Get

The Coaching Report — Four Bands, One Read

Every report opens with a pastoral read of the whole, then walks through four sections: where the sermon stood across ten dimensions, what worked, what to work on next, and the eight signature craft moves a preacher can carry into the week.

A Read of the Whole

Synthesis Paragraph

Before the numbers — what did the sermon try to do, what did it do well, where did the weight sit. A pastoral 4-6 sentence frame, anchored in the actual sermon.

Dimensions

Ten Scores with Evidence

Ten dimensions across four tiers — Foundation, Craft, Connection, Style. Each scored 1-5 with confidence, plain commentary, and a direct quote from the transcript.

What's Working

Four to Five Affirmations

The strongest moves the preacher made in this sermon. Title, observation, quote — anchored in rubric vocabulary so the same labels can show up across weeks.

Things to Work On

Three Priorities for Next Week

The three highest-impact things to work on next. Each card carries Observation, Recommendation, and a Practicum — one specific small thing to try this week in prep.

Signatures

Eight Craft Moves Observed

Christological turn, intertextual anchoring, aphoristic compression, identity framing in the call, and four others — observed and quoted, or noted as absent without penalty.

Speaker & Date

Trackable Across Sermons

Each report carries the speaker name and sermon date so reports stack week-to-week and growth on a dimension or signature becomes visible across cycles.
